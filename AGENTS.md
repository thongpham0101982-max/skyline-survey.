## Sky-Line Frontend Design System (`frontend-design` Skill)
When creating or updating any page or component in `src/app/` or `src/components/`, always follow the `.agents/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md` guidelines and the **Sky-Line Academic Ledger** aesthetic:
1. **Palette:** Anchor layouts with Sky-Line Deep Pine (`#003B3A`), Brand Teal (`#00A19A` / `#00736E`), and crisp Slate/Alabaster surfaces (`#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders). Reserve Emerald (`#059669`), Amber (`#D97706`), and Rose (`#E11D48`) strictly for semantic statuses.
2. **Typography:** Use clean Vietnamese sentence-case labels for table headers (`th`), form fields (`label`), and status badges. Always apply `tabular-nums` to tables, metrics, scores, timestamps, and IDs.
3. **Restraint:** Avoid decorative `animate-ping` dots on static headers, redundant filler badges on charts, and excessive card hover-lift effects.
